# Build a Premium, Fully Functional Website for OPTIVA

## 1. About Our Company

Our company is called **OPTIVA**.

OPTIVA is a professional web design and website development agency based in Georgia, serving businesses in Georgia and international markets.

We create modern, responsive, professional websites for businesses that want to establish an online presence and attract customers.

Our target customers include:
- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Restaurants and cafés
- Beauty salons and barbershops
- Retail stores
- Startups and new businesses
- Professional service providers
- Other businesses that need a website

The main objective is to turn website visitors into paying customers by helping them understand our services, compare packages, and contact us directly.

This is a real business website, not a demo or template showcase.

## 2. Overall Design

I want a premium, visually impressive, modern corporate website.

The website should feel like it was designed by an experienced professional design agency, not automatically generated.

Design requirements:

- Elegant black, white, and charcoal color palette, with subtle blue accents.
- Sophisticated typography with excellent readability.
- Beautiful layouts with carefully balanced spacing.
- High-quality typography, buttons, icons, and navigation.
- Smooth scrolling and subtle animations.
- Professional hover effects and transitions.
- Carefully designed service and pricing cards.
- Clean, responsive design across all screen sizes.
- Premium, distinctive branding for OPTIVA.

Avoid generic AI website aesthetics, excessive gradients, oversized emojis, repetitive layouts, and unnecessary decorative sections.

The overall feeling should be modern, trustworthy, international, and professional.

Our brand name OPTIVA should be clearly visible in the header and footer.
